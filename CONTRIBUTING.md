# Contributing Guidelines

By contributing to this repository, you agree to strictly follow the rules below. **Pull Requests (PRs) or Issues that do not adhere to these guidelines will be closed immediately without review.**

## 1. No Unsolicited Pull Requests
* **Do not open a Pull Request without an approved Issue first.**
* Before writing any code, open an Issue to propose your change or bug fix and wait for explicit maintainer approval.
* Unsolicited PRs, "drive-by" refactoring, formatting-only changes, or minor typo fixes will be rejected and closed.

## 2. Strict Scope & Quality Standards
* **No Bloat:** Do not introduce new dependencies, libraries, or unnecessary abstractions unless explicitly approved.
* **Local-First Principle:** This project operates strictly locally. Any contribution that introduces telemetry, analytics, remote server calls, or external tracking will be **permanently rejected**.
* **Code Quality:** Code must be clean, minimal, and strictly match the existing style and architecture of the repository.
* **No AI Slop:** Low-effort, unreviewed, or AI-generated PRs and Issues will result in an immediate ban from the repository.

## 3. Testing & Verification
* You are responsible for thoroughly testing your changes locally before submitting.
* Do not submit broken, untested, or incomplete code.
* Clearly document what was changed, why, and how it was tested in your PR description.

## 4. Issue Reporting Rules
* Search existing Issues before opening a new one. Duplicates will be closed.
* Bug reports must include exact steps to reproduce, your environment/OS details, and expected vs. actual behavior. Vague reports ("it doesn't work") will be closed without response.

## 5. Maintainer Discretion & Rights
* Maintainers reserve the absolute right to reject, close, or modify any Issue or Pull Request at any time, for any reason, without obligation to provide an explanation.
* Submitting a contribution does not guarantee its inclusion, nor does it grant you any ownership or decision-making rights over the project.
* All accepted contributions are subject to the project's license and terms.
